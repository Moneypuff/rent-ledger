Basis. One input is taken as given: the $2,310.00 biweekly salary. Every other figure on this page is computed from the published 2026 rate tables, not inferred from a payroll calculator's output. Each rate is cross-checked against its published annual maximum.

Rounding. Each period is rounded to the cent independently and annual totals are the sum of those rounded periods, which is how source deductions are actually remitted. This can differ by a cent or two from rate × annual earnings.

Check before you remit. Rates and ceilings change every January. Due dates here roll forward off weekends but do not account for statutory holidays. Confirm the figures against PDOC and your remitter frequency — the CRA and Revenu Québec assign those independently, so yours may not both be monthly.
