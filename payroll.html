Québec splits your remittances two ways. Federal income tax and EI (employee + employer) go to the CRA. QPP, QPIP and Québec income tax go to Revenu Québec — they are not part of your CRA remittance. Both ledgers are below.

Parties

Pay schedule

Biweekly = every 14 days from the first pay date. New employers are regular (monthly) remitters by default; quarterly is only available once the CRA grants it.

Pay & income tax

Salary is fixed per period regardless of hours worked. Income tax is a flat per-period amount because the salary and TD1 claim don't change during the year. PDOC does not calculate Québec provincial tax — get it from Revenu Québec's WebRAS and enter it above, otherwise the Québec column stays at zero.

Statutory rates

QPP and EI rates are derived from your PDOC result and reproduce it to the cent. QPIP (RQAP) is not shown by PDOC, so it defaults to 0 — enter the current Revenu Québec rates to include it in the provincial remittance.
